# Specification

## Overview

A Gridfinity compatible openscad generator for making miniature holders and and bins to hold the holders. Technically, it's making a compatability layer between two systems. 


## Disclaimer

I've only ever used openscad designs, never made my own. This'll be interesting. 

## Non Goals

There's plenty of gridfinity holders that work for miniatures using magnets, I don't want magnets.
